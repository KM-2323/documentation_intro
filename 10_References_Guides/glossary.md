## Short glossary

**Adiabatic states:** eigenstates of the electronic Hamiltonian at fixed nuclear geometry.

**Diabatic states:** transformed electronic basis intended to vary smoothly with nuclear geometry and minimise/remove derivative couplings.

**Quasi-diabatic states:** practical finite-subspace approximation to diabatic states.

**NACV:** nonadiabatic coupling vector, usually \(\F_{ij}=\braket{\psi_i}{\nabla\psi_j}\).

**NACT:** nonadiabatic coupling term; often used similarly to NACV, sometimes component-wise or matrix-wise.

**NACM:** nonadiabatic coupling matrix, the matrix whose elements are NACTs/NACVs.

**ADT matrix:** adiabatic-to-diabatic transformation matrix.

**LVC model:** linear vibronic coupling model, using first-order expansions of diabatic potential matrix elements around a reference geometry or CI point.

**Branching space / g-h plane:** two-dimensional subspace spanned locally by the gradient-difference and coupling vectors; displacements in this space lift the degeneracy.

**Intersection space / seam:** orthogonal complement to the branching space; degeneracy persists to first order along this space.

**Tuning coordinate:** coordinate changing the diagonal energy gap.

**Coupling coordinate:** coordinate generating the off-diagonal diabatic coupling.

**Peaked vs sloped CI:** classification depending on relative gradient-sum and gradient-difference behaviour; peaked if the intersection is effectively the upper-surface minimum, sloped if tilted downhill.
