$N_s$: Number of electronic states
$N_{\text{nuc}}$: Number of atoms/nuclei 
$f$: Nuclear degreee of freedom (tend to refer to internal coordiantes/normal modes  = $3N_{\text{nuc}}-6 \text{ or } 3N_{\text{nuc}}-5$ for non-linear and linear molecules respetcively)
$\ket{\psi_i}$: Adiabatic electronic basis
$\psivec$: Adiabatic electronic vector (row vector)
$\ket{\varphi_i}$: Diabatic/quasi-diabatic electronic basis
$\varphivec$: Diabatic/quasi-diabatic electronic vector (row vector)
$\chivec$: Nuclear wavefunction vector
$\W$: Diabatic potential matrix
$\V$: Adiabatic potential matrix
$\F$: non-adiabatic coupling vector/tensor $\in N_s \times N_s \times f$ 
$\G$: non-adiabatic coupling scalar/matrix (also known as the second-order scalar coupling) $\in N_s \times N_s $
$\Cmat$: Adiabatic-to-diabatic (ADT) matrix defines as 

$$
\W = \Cinv \V \Cmat,\\quad \varphivec = \psivec \Cmat
$$
