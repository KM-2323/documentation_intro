As introduced in the CASSCF basic theory, the CASSCF method is a multi-configurational SCF method (MCSCF). This implies that the electronic wavefunctions are composed of configuration state functions (CSFs), formed from populating the orbitals in different ways. Within a selected active space, by specifying the number of inactive and active orbitals, the number of electrons, and the spin multiplicity, all possible configurations are generated by distributing electrons among the active orbitals while maintaining the specified spin symmetry and keeping the inactive orbitals fully occupied. 

Suppose there are $N_I$ inactive orbital, populated by $2N_I$ electrons, will be treated as non-correlated. Then, let the number of active orbitals be $N_A$ and are populated by $n_A$ electrons. The number of spin-pure CSFs, using Weyl's formula, is:

$$
N_{CSF} =\frac{2S+1}{N_A+1}\begin{pmatrix}N_A+1 \\ \frac{n_A}{2}-S\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}N_A+1 \\ \frac{n_A}{2}+S+1\end{pmatrix}
$$

Here the notation $\begin{pmatrix}N \\ k\end{pmatrix} = \frac{N!}{k!(N-k)!}$
