# Two-state loop and sign change

## Purpose

Use the two-state conical intersection model to show how $\mat D$ arises.

## Two-state LVC model

Recall

$$
x=\Delta,
\qquad
y=2W_{12}.
$$

## Mixing angle

Use

$$
\theta=-\frac{1}{2}\operatorname{atan2}(y,x)
$$

or the project convention.

## Loop around the origin

Parametrise

$$
x=\rho\cos\phi,
\qquad
y=\rho\sin\phi.
$$

## Change in mixing angle

Show that after $\phi:0\to 2\pi$, the mixing angle changes by $\pi$ up to sign convention.

## Topological matrix

Show the corresponding $\mat D$.

## Diabatic potential remains single-valued

Explain why the sign change is acceptable.
