# Three-state sign flips and topological matrices

## Purpose

Show how sign flips combine in a three-state real electronic basis.

## Three-state diagonal $\mat D$ matrices

Define possible sign matrices:

$$
\mat D_{12}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,-1,+1),
$$

$$
\mat D_{23}=\operatorname{diag}(+1,-1,-1).
$$

## Loop around one seam

Interpret $\mat D_{12}$.

## Loop around another seam

Interpret $\mat D_{23}$.

## Loop around multiple seams

Discuss products of sign matrices.

## Geometric interpretation

The loop transports an electronic frame. The sign pattern records how that frame returns.

## Caveat

This diagonal sign picture is appropriate for real states and separated pairwise topological events. General multistate degeneracies can be non-Abelian.
