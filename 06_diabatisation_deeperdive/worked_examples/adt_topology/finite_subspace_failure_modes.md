# Finite-subspace failure modes in ADT construction

## Purpose

Show why omitted states matter.

## Toy retained two-state system

Define a two-state retained $P$-space.

## Add weak external coupling

Introduce a third state in $Q$ with coupling $O(\epsilon)$.

## Effect on curl

Show schematically that the retained curl condition is violated at small but nonzero order.

## Effect on path dependence

Two paths between the same endpoints give slightly different transformations.

## Effect on $\mat D$

Closed-loop matrices become only approximately diagonal or approximately quantised.

## Interpretation

The remedy is not to force the two-state model. The remedy is to enlarge the retained state space or restrict the calculation to regions where the omitted-state coupling remains negligible.
