# Reduced sub-Hilbert-space errors in ADT theory

## Purpose

Derive or summarise how omitted-state couplings perturb the retained ADT construction.

## Define $P$ and $Q$ spaces

Use

$$
\hat P_M+\hat Q_M=\hat I_{\mathrm{el}}.
$$

## Coupling assumption

$$
\F_{ia}=O(\epsilon),
\qquad
i\in P,\quad a\in Q.
$$

## Projected derivative coupling field

Define the retained coupling field $\F^{(P)}$.

## Missing terms in the retained curl condition

Show schematically that the curl condition in the retained subspace receives corrections from omitted-state couplings.

## Perturbation order

State carefully that if omitted couplings are $O(\epsilon)$, then many retained-subspace errors enter at $O(\epsilon^2)$.

## Consequence for $\Cmat^{(P)}$

The ADT matrix is approximate and may become path-dependent at the same order.

## Consequence for $\W^{(P)}$

The diabatic potential matrix is quasi-diabatic rather than exact.
