# Condition for single-valued diabatic potentials from the topological matrix

## Starting point

$$
\W=\Cmat^\dagger \V \Cmat.
$$

## ADT matrix after a closed loop

Assume after a closed loop,

$$
\Cmat_{\mathrm{final}}
=
\Cmat_{\mathrm{initial}}\mat D.
$$

## Diabatic potential after the loop

Substitute into $\W$:

$$
\W_{\mathrm{final}}
=
\mat D^\dagger
\W_{\mathrm{initial}}
\mat D
$$

or the equivalent expression depending on convention.

## Single-valuedness condition

Require

$$
\W_{\mathrm{final}}=\W_{\mathrm{initial}}.
$$

## Nondegenerate real case

Show why $\mat D$ must be diagonal with entries $\pm 1$.

## Degenerate-block caveat

If states are exactly degenerate, $\mat D$ may be block unitary within a degenerate subspace. State this as an advanced caveat.

## Interpretation

The ADT matrix may change sign or phase, but the diabatic potential must return to itself.
